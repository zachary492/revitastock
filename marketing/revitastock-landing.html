Two ways your inventory quietly goes wrong

Neither shows up as an error. They just cost you, silently, until you catch them by accident.

Ghost listing

The item's gone from your shelf, but the listing's still live. Someone buys it anyway — now you're canceling the order, eating an out-of-stock defect, and hoping it doesn't tip your seller standing.

Phantom drop

The item's sitting right there, but the listing shows zero. No error, no warning — just a sale that never happens because nobody could buy what they couldn't see.
