trim the pauses if you need it tighter)

seller, not a corporate pitch

## Full Voiceover (read straight through, ~35 sec at a natural pace)

> Your item sold out three days ago. Your eBay listing doesn't know that.
> It's called a ghost listing — and it's costing sellers order
> cancellations and account defects, every single day. RevitaStock
> catches the mismatch between your shelf and your store, before it
> turns into a penalty. Upload your count. Connect your store. Get your
> fix list. That's it. Built for the sellers everyone else forgot — auto
> parts, thrift, vintage, one-of-a-kind. Back RevitaStock today — link
> below.

## Production notes

- **The screen-recording segments (0:08–0:26) can be captured directly
  from the landing page I built you** — just open it in a browser,
  scroll slowly, and screen-record. The ledger widget already animates
  itself, so you can time the recording to catch the ghost-listing tag
  flipping to "Fixed" naturally.
- **The animated-text segments (0:00–0:08, 0:26–0:31) don't need the
  product at all** — any simple text-animation tool works: Canva,
  CapCut, or an AI video tool like Pictory if you're voicing it with
  synthetic narration.
- **Keep the claims honest.** Nothing in this script promises the
  product is finished or already has customers — it's positioned as "in
  development, back us to help it happen," which matches where you
  actually are. Don't let an editor or AI tool add fake stats, user
  counts, or "as seen in" logos when assembling this.
- **Music:** something low-key and a little tense under the first half
  (the problem), resolving to something more confident under the second
  half (the solution) — reinforces the "before/after" structure without
  needing extra words.
- **Captions:** burn in captions regardless of platform — most people
  watch crowdfunding video previews muted first.
